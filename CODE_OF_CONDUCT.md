# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests and
discussions, agrees to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Expected: be considerate, accept constructive criticism, focus on what is best
for the project, and show empathy toward other members.

Not accepted: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, personal or political attacks, harassment, publishing others'
private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

Maintainers remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban any participant for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Report abuse by email to conduct@flopwire.com. Every report is reviewed by the
maintainer and the reporter's privacy is kept.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
